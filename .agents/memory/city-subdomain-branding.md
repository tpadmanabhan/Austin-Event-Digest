Do not display the legacy EventCarpooling logo in city subdomain page headers or footers; keep the city-specific marks. All city landing pages, full editions, and weekly digest templates should avoid visible EventCarpooling/carpooling references and legacy logo assets. Mention IRL (In Real Life) where it fits naturally.

**Why:** the user requested removing EventCarpooling/carpooling references from city landing pages, full editions, and newsletter templates, and mentioning IRL where suitable.

**How to apply:** Preserve this rule when changing shared city layouts, full-edition pages, or weekly digest templates. Keep city identities primary and use IRL as platform attribution where appropriate; leave operational RSVP/admin copy, event-submitted content, and sender-address configuration outside this scope.

The approved standalone IRL mark uses a deep-green circular badge, white “IRL” lettering, an orange accent, and an “In Real Life” tagline.

**Why:** the user approved this visual concept.

**How to apply:** Reuse this direction for future IRL brand asset refinements. Approval of the standalone concept does not by itself replace the existing site or city logos.
