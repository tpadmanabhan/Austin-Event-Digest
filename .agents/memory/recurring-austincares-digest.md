---
name: Recurring AustinCares digest offers
description: Choosing digest date labels for ongoing weekly deals and services
---

Use a label without a weekday or month/day, such as "Weekly offer", for recurring AustinCares digest entries; put the actual operating day(s) and hours in the title or description.

**Why:** The digest client resolves weekday-only labels against the edition's week and hides them after that day passes. A recurring restaurant special or city service would then vanish from an otherwise useful weekly edition.

**How to apply:** For ongoing deals and services, preserve the weekly schedule in descriptive text and keep entries in day order when curating the edition. For one-time events, use a real calendar date instead. This does not make offers verified indefinitely; check availability before future sends.
