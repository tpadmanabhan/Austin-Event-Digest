---
name: Public source facts versus edition labels
description: Why source-verified landing-page metadata must not rewrite historical curation
---

Keep original newsletter records and date labels when adding source-verified facts for public landing pages. Prefer verified source fields on the event detail page, without rewriting the edition just to qualify for structured data.

**Why:** A live organizer's precise start can differ from the older newsletter label. Source enrichment supports factual landing pages without erasing historical curation. Unknown years, times and addresses must not be synthesized.

**How to apply:** Enrich only when the source occurrence and venue are demonstrably the same. Leave unsupported or unavailable facts out of Event structured data. Keep subscriber actions private and preserve their exact original inputs independently of new public URLs.
